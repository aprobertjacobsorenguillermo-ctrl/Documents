The Problem

Many Filipinos face money problems like debt, overspending, and falling for scams because schools do not teach financial discipline in the curriculum. Root cause: Lack of lessons on budgeting, saving, and wise consumer choices in TLE/FCS classes.

Effects: High household debt, family stress, poverty cycles. According to Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, low financial literacy affects millions of Filipinos.

How It Works

This tool teaches financial discipline in FCS class by dividing money into two accounts:

  • Needs Account (50-70%): For essentials like food, school supplies, bills.
  • Savings/Wants Account (30-50%): For savings and controlled fun spending.

Automation: Set up auto-transfers (e.g., 20% of allowance to savings via GCash or bank app) to build saving habits. Steps:

  1. Open two accounts (or use digital wallets like GCash).
  2. Set a percentage rule (e.g., 60% needs, 40% savings/wants).
  3. Automate transfers on payday/allowance day.
  4. Track weekly and adjust as needed.

This addresses the root cause by giving hands-on practice in financial discipline schools miss. Measurable: Track savings growth in class simulations.
